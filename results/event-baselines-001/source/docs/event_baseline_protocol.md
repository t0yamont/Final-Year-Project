# Event-model baseline pilot protocol — 30 October 2026

Frozen before executing the new baseline study. Dependencies: event-validation-002 numerical/physical/analytic gate and unchanged solver hashes. The study implements guide Step 10 on the accepted synthetic event model. It does not establish a calibrated car, full-circuit benchmark or final held-out experiment.

Use identical event transitions and constraints for all methods. State is segment index, usable-energy bin and exact reachable speed. Energy is floored relative to the reserve; speed is not rounded or replaced with its cap. Solve Bellman recursion with cached transitions and states. Replay selected actions from the original unrounded state. The rounded objective is not asserted to bound the physical replay. The finite action tree limits scale; this is not production full-lap DP.

On the previously frozen two-segment nominal and new-evaluation scenarios, compare zero/half/full, exhaustive five actions {0,.25,.5,.75,1}, energy-grid DP at 20/10/5/2.5 kJ, and exhaustive nine equally spaced actions. Action refinement is separate from energy refinement. Reuse saved PPO evaluations without retraining or selecting checkpoints. These evaluation scenarios have now been inspected and cannot become untouched final tests.

Also introduce a separately labelled four-segment synthetic development stress case: lengths [120,280,160,240] m, exit caps [32,20,28,20] m/s, initial speed 20 m/s, energy 450 kJ, reserve 50 kJ and default PowerVehicle. Use three actions {0,.5,1}, exhaustive 81 sequences and DP at 20/10/5/2.5 kJ. This case tests allocation across additional decisions; it is not used to choose among existing PPO checkpoints and will have no PPO comparison in this increment.

Acceptance: all reported policies replay feasibly (reserve and speed caps within 1e-6, work residual within .01 J); DP replay cannot beat the matching exhaustive finite-action reference by more than 1e-5 s. Record every DP replay gap and rounded objective without assuming monotonic refinement. A final gap <=1e-4 s is the predeclared finite-grid agreement threshold; failure keeps the DP reference gate open. Require coarse/fine integration agreement <=1e-5 s for the chosen exhaustive policy on each scenario, separately from action/state discretisation. Nine-action results are a sensitivity diagnostic, not proof of a continuous optimum.

Pilot limit: at most 2,000 uncached transitions per scenario, 120 seconds per scenario and 300 seconds in total for the benchmark. Stop with a saved failure record if exceeded; do not relabel partial results as a pass. Record solve/replay and verification times, cache hits, dependency hashes, versions and all actual commands. Produce PNG (300 dpi), vector PDF, captions and a manifest from saved metrics. No new network requests or PPO training are necessary for this gate.
