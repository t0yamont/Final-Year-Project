## Changes to the research plan

These papers support strengthening the existing protocol, rather than replacing the simulator or launching another training campaign. Before final controller comparisons, freeze initial/terminal conditions, scenario pairing, information available to each controller, tuning budgets and numerical tolerances. Separate parameter uncertainty, numerical error and variation between training seeds. A small lap-time difference is not convincing if these uncertainties are larger.

The immediate scientific bottlenecks remain action-grid convergence, a defensible full-circuit model and independent empirical validation. The current data position and staged acquisition plan are in [data_sufficiency.md](data_sufficiency.md). Core reconstruction and controller comparisons take priority; mixed-session and profile-conditioning claims require their own evidence rather than being inferred from the core result.
